    What your taste says about you

    An interpretation, not a fact sheet: what this mix of shows suggests, read the way a friend might.

    Long-form over soundbites

    The Joe Rogan Experience (415.9 h) and Lex Fridman (90.9 h) are three-hour conversations, and Hardcore History episodes run longer still. That suggests someone who would rather hear an idea argued out than summarized.

    Builder brain

    All-In (224.5 h), Acquired, Invest Like the Best, Cheeky Pint, The a16z Show and AI + a16z point to a tech, startup and markets lens: how companies get built and where AI is heading.

    Reads the whole board

    Breaking Points, The Tucker Carlson Show, The Daily, The Journal. and The President’s Daily Brief come from very different camps. One reading: you like to hear a story from several sides before deciding.

    History as a lens

    Dan Carlin’s Hardcore History, WhatifAlthist and History That Doesn’t Suck suggest you treat the past as a way to understand the present.

    Curious about the edges

    Darknet Diaries, American Alchemy and Duncan Trussell Family Hour add hackers, fringe science and the cosmic. You clearly enjoy a rabbit hole.

    Laughs included

    KILL TONY and This Past Weekend w/ Theo Von keep it loose between the heavy topics.

    The kind of people who tend to share this mix

    • Founders, engineers and operators who learn on the commute or between meetings.
    • Independent thinkers who pick “long interview” over “hot take.”
    • History buffs who would happily spend six hours on one war.
    • AI-curious tinkerers tracking where the tools are going next.

    A generalization drawn from the shows, not a claim about any specific person.
