The crowd, measured

Where young, athletic women actually are

No single statistic measures this, so the crowd score combines seven public measures for the county or counties each trip would happen in. Each measure is turned into a 0–10 rank across all 29 destinations, then weighted: athleisure stores 20%, boutique studios 20%, women aged 21–34 20%, nightlife 15%, median age 10%, fitness rank 10%, college enrollment 5%. Where a measure doesn't exist for a place (fitness rank outside the 100 largest U.S. cities, for example), the others are reweighted.

Denver tops itIt leads on studios per resident (4.7 per 100,000), ranks 5th of 100 on the ACSM fitness index, and 12.7% of residents are women aged 21–34. Austin is a tenth of a point behind, and Nashville has the highest share of young women of any U.S. destination (13.6%).

Resort towns sinkHilton Head, Myrtle Beach and Pinehurst have median ages of 43 to 49 and almost no athleisure retail. Great golf, wrong crowd. That single fact moves Pinehurst from a golf pilgrimage to 24th.

Read Mexico carefullyLos Cabos and the Riviera Maya score well because their residents are very young (median age 27–28) and, in Cabo, one Lululemon and one Alo serve a small population. The census counts residents, many of them resort workers, not who's at the beach club.

1

Austin & the Hill Country

To par−11

The best all-round answer, and the only place where you can hunt from a helicopter.

Austin is second of 29 on the crowd score (8.7): 12.1% of Travis County residents are women aged 21–34, the median age is 35.6, it's 28th on the ACSM fitness index, sixth in the country for nightlife, and 134,000 college students live within 30 miles. Add a 3¾-hour average flight and better-than-average golf, house and weird scores, and it wins by seven strokes. The golf is good rather than great, and the best of it sits behind a resort-guest rule you can solve by holding a couple of hotel rooms.

Golf
Omni Barton Creek, Fazio Canyons (#20 in Texas per GOLF) and Fazio Foothills, both former Golf Digest 100 Greatest Public. Registered Omni guests only; estimated $250–300. Public fallback: Lost Pines Golf Club (formerly Wolfdancer; Arthur Hills, slope 141, about $150 on weekends), 45–50 minutes east and on the way to the airport.
House
Lake Austin: 25–35 minutes to downtown, 15 to Barton Creek. Closest to yours: ABOVE "Elite Estate" on Lake Austin (7 BR, sleeps 16–17, pool and spa, basketball/pickleball court, theater, sand volleyball, boat slip). The splurge: Escondido Chateau (Vrbo 490043), 10 BR on 12.6 acres with 800 ft of Lake Travis frontage, infinity pool, hot tub, two lit pickleball courts, a basketball court and a yacht dock, about $3,803/night, but an hour from downtown.
Crowd
Rainey Street bungalow bars, West 6th for young professionals, the White Horse honky-tonk, and Devil's Cove on Lake Travis, where about 200 boats raft up on Saturdays.
Weird
Helicopter hog hunting out of Temple or Bryan (feral hogs are an invasive pest in Texas, and shooting them from the air is legal). Full-auto at The Range at Austin. Two-step lessons at the Broken Spoke, open since 1964. Gentlemen's clubs: Yellow Rose, Sugar's, Perfect 10 (check they're still operating).
Window
Oct 14–17, 2027 sits between Austin City Limits (expected Oct 1–3 and 8–10) and the F1 U.S. Grand Prix (Oct 22–24). Late March–April also works.
Flights
BOS 4h40 · DC 3h32 · EWR 4h14 · ATL 2h33, all nonstop
The catch
The best courses are resort-gated. COTA's public driving days end in November 2026, when the track becomes a private club. Many big houses ban "bachelor parties," and Austin caps noise at 75 dB.
T2

Charleston

To par−4

The best round in the report, a young downtown, and two hours from home.

Charleston's crowd score (6.5) is 7th of 29. 10.5% of residents are women aged 21–34, King Street and the College of Charleston keep downtown young, and the county has more boutique studios per resident than San Diego. What lifts it to a tie for second is everything else: the Ocean Course, 78° Octobers, and the shortest flights of any destination, about two hours on average from your four cities.

Golf
The Ocean Course at Kiawah (Pete Dye; the 1991 Ryder Cup and the 2012 and 2021 PGA Championships; est. $450–600 plus caddie). Wild Dunes Links on Isle of Palms (Tom Fazio's first design, with an Atlantic finish). Also Osprey Point (Fazio) and Turtle Point (Nicklaus) on Kiawah. The Ocean Course is about an hour from Isle of Palms, the one round that breaks the 45-minute rule.
House
Isle of Palms: 25 minutes to King Street, beach out the back door. Closest match: an 8-bedroom, 9-bath oceanview house with a private pool, 8-person hot tub and elevator, steps to the beach (Expedia/Travelocity h73766421). Isle of Palms caps overnight guests at 12, which is fine for 8.
Crowd
Upper King Street at night, the Shem Creek boardwalk at sunset, beach volleyball at the Windjammer on Isle of Palms, Folly Beach's Center Street.
Weird
Sporting clays and quail on 15,000 acres at Back Woods Quail Club. Gulf Stream fishing (two boats; six per boat). A captained sandbar day to Morris Island Lighthouse.
Window
October. Hurricane season technically runs to Nov 30, but mid-October risk is low.
Flights
BOS 2h38 · DC 1h49 · EWR 2h17 · ATL 1h14, all nonstop
The catch
No rental here has a basketball court. Nightlife is smaller and ends earlier than Austin's, and the Ocean Course is a long morning drive from the beach house.
T2

Denver & Boulder

To par−4

The number-one crowd on the list, and the cheapest trip.

Denver and Boulder have the highest crowd score of all 29 destinations (8.8). They are 5th of 100 on the ACSM fitness index, first on boutique studios per resident (Solidcore, Barry's, CorePower and F45 are everywhere), and 12.7% of residents are women aged 21–34, with 80,000 college students in range. Mid-September weather is perfect. It gives back ground on golf (good public courses, nothing at destination level) and on the house, because Denver only allows short-term rentals in the owner's primary residence.

Golf
Fossil Trace in Golden (Jim Engh; $105–120 in fall, cart included; 15 min). CommonGround (Tom Doak; $95 plus $23 cart; book 6 days out; 35 min). Arrowhead (Robert Trent Jones Jr., among red sandstone fins; about $215; check its bunker renovation).
House
West suburbs, 20 minutes from LoDo. Best verified: an 8-bedroom house in Arvada sleeping 20, with a hot tub and two fire pits, about $900/night (Vrbo 3122219). No pool, no court, renters 26 and up. No legal Denver-area 8-bedroom rental with a pool and court turned up.
Crowd
LoDo and Union Station, LoHi, RiNo, Larimer Square; Temple for EDM. WalletHub ranks Denver 11th for nightlife and 5th for singles.
Weird
A private full-auto range in Loveland: Phoenix Weaponry's group packages run $1,837 (6 guns, 1,250 rounds) to $2,254 (8 guns). A show at Red Rocks. Indian Hot Springs in Idaho Springs ($20–36). Colorado's licensed psilocybin healing centers exist but involve screening and prep, so they're not a weekend activity.
Window
Mid-September to mid-October. Snow is possible from October.
Flights
BOS 4h44 · DC 4h05 · EWR 4h29 · ATL 3h23, all nonstop
The catch
No mansion with a pool and court, and no bucket-list round. You're trading golf and house for the crowd.
4

Los Cabos

To par−3

Passport required

The best golf on the list, and staffed villas.

Cabo's crowd score (7.1) ties Charlotte for fourth of 29, but read it carefully: Los Cabos' residents are very young (median age 27, and 13.6% are women aged 20–34) and one Lululemon and one Alo serve a population of 351,000. The census measures the people who live and work there, not who's at Taboo on Saturday. On everything else it's excellent: a PGA Tour course, villas with a chef and bartender, and dry 84° days by mid-November. Travel costs it the most: Boston has no nonstop.

Golf
Diamante Dunes (Davis Love III; ranked #1 in Mexico) and El Cardonal (Tiger Woods; home of the PGA Tour's World Wide Technology Championship). You can only play them by renting a privately owned Diamante unit for at least three nights, which a Thursday–Sunday trip meets exactly; about $535 a round. Open to anyone: Solmar Golf Links (Greg Norman, $395) and the Cabo del Sol Course (Weiskopf).
House
Closest to yours: Villa Chavez in Pedregal (7–8 BR, sleeps 18, saltwater pool, spa, a private tennis/pickleball court, from about $3,000/night). Most staff: Villa Lands End, with a chef, bartender and "nightlife host" included. Skip Mantea Casa Cabo: 7-night minimum, and all-male groups must pay for a 24-hour butler.
Crowd
Medano Beach by day (Taboo, Mango Deck, SUR Beach House, Bagatelle, Mandala), El Squid Roe and Cabo Wabo at night. Cabo was the #2 international spring-break destination in 2026.
Weird
Striped marlin season opens in November (full-day charters $445–3,500 per boat). A private yacht to El Arco runs about $1,500 for eight. UTVs, ziplines and bungee at Wild Canyon ($298). Whale sharks in La Paz, October–May. Gentlemen's clubs: Splash, Mermaids.
Window
Thu Nov 11 – Sun Nov 14, 2027, or early December. Avoid August–October (hurricane season peaks in September; Bisbee's marlin tournament fills the marina in late October).
Flights
BOS 8h29 (one stop) · DC 5h38 (IAD, 4/wk) · EWR 6h14 (4/wk) · ATL 4h27
The catch
No shooting of any kind. Mexico has no tourist ranges, and bringing in even one round of ammunition can mean 5–30 years in prison, so check every golf bag. The best courses are gated. The State Department rates Baja California Sur Level 2 (exercise increased caution).

The Irish option

Links, whiskey and a red-eye

You asked for Ireland, so I scored the two best Irish versions of this trip the same way as everything else. Both have world-class links golf. Both lose points on 57–62° fall weather and the overnight flight. Under the crowd-weighted scoring, Dublin finishes 17th and the Causeway Coast 28th, last of 28. If Ireland is the call anyway, do Dublin, and do it in early September.

28

The Causeway Coast

To par+29

The best golf in Ireland, ten minutes from the oldest licensed distillery.

Stay out of Ireland during Ryder Cup week

The 2027 Ryder Cup is at Adare Manor, September 13–19. General admission sold out after a ballot that was more than 12 times oversubscribed, and resale is banned. What's left is hospitality from €450–600 per person plus VAT, or packages from €4,598 for five nights with a 90-minute bus each way. Tee times and houses across the country get squeezed that week, which is why the Dublin plan is the weekend before.

A note on "Scotch"

Scotch only comes from Scotland. Ireland makes Irish whiskey, spelled with an "e," usually triple-distilled, and "single pot still" is a style only Ireland makes. Order a Redbreast and you'll fit right in.

If the group really means Scotch, the Scottish version of this trip is St Andrews plus a day in Speyside. Say the word and I'll score it.

Fine print

What I ruled out, and why

You said everything was on the table. Some of it doesn't survive contact with the law or a four-day schedule.

Ayahuasca: not on this trip

Reputable centers sell multi-night programs only (Soltara's minimum is 5 nights, Rythmia's 7). They screen out people on SSRIs and with some heart conditions, and each ceremony runs all night. The one-night ceremonies sold in Tulum aren't vetted. If the group is serious, make it a separate trip. For a legal session that fits a weekend, Bend's licensed psilocybin centers are the option.

Guns in Mexico: none

Mexico has no tourist shooting ranges, and bringing in even one round of ammunition can mean 5 to 30 years in prison. Check every golf bag and jacket pocket before Cabo or Playa. All the full-auto in this report is in Nevada, Texas and Florida.

California: no machine guns

Civilian full-auto rentals aren't available in San Diego or Palm Springs. Poway Weapons & Gear has 42 lanes of ordinary shooting if someone insists.

Rental rules are the new bouncer

Las Vegas wants a licensed home and usually bans outdoor music. Miami Beach bans rentals under six months in single-family homes. Austin caps noise at 75 dB, and many listings ban "bachelor parties." Denver only allows rentals in the host's primary residence, and Newport Beach has a permit waitlist. Isle of Palms allows 12 overnight guests. Tell hosts the truth: it's an annual reunion of grad-school friends.

Desert courses close to overseed

Desert courses close or go cart-path-only for a couple of weeks each fall to overseed (SilverRock was closed Oct 12–30, 2026). Before you lock Vegas dates, ask Cascata and Wynn for their 2027 schedule.

Vegas pools close in late October

Encore Beach Club, Tao Beach and LIV Beach close in late October, and OMNIA's dayclub at the end of September. Circa's Stadium Swim stays open all year. That's why the Vegas plan is Oct 14–17.

Shadow Creek doesn't fit

Public play is Monday to Thursday, for guests of MGM hotels, at $1,250 with a limo and caddie. On a Thursday-arrival trip, it would mean playing straight off the plane.

Massage: licensed only

Book through an app like Soothe, which sends licensed therapists to the house in Las Vegas, Austin, San Diego and Nashville. It doesn't serve Charleston.

How the scores work

Twenty-nine destinations get six scores out of 10, and none is dropped: the cut marks the top ten and ties, and everything below it still shows a score and a short profile. Default weights are crowd 40%, golf 20%, and house, weather, weird and travel 10% each. "To par" is the gap to the Scottsdale house under the same weights and month, at one stroke per tenth of a point.

Crowd combines seven measures for the county or counties each trip would happen in. Each is converted to a 0–10 percentile rank across the 29, then weighted. Athleisure stores per 100,000 residents (Lululemon, Alo, Vuori) and boutique studios per 100,000 (Barry's, Solidcore, Orangetheory, F45, CorePower, Club Pilates) are 20% each. The share of the population that is women aged 21–34 is 20%. Nightlife is 15%. Median age (younger is better) and the ACSM fitness rank are 10% each. College enrollment within 30 miles, on a log scale, is 5%. A missing measure is dropped and the rest reweighted. The full table and sources are in the crowd section.

Weather is calculated from 1991–2020 monthly normals. Highs of 74–84°F get full marks. Each degree hotter costs 0.6, and humid places count 1–3°F hotter above 80. Each degree cooler costs 0.45, and each degree a low falls under 50°F costs 0.12. Rainy or hurricane months cost 0.3–3 more, and so do months when mountain courses are closed. Travel is the average outbound flight from Boston, DC, Newark and Atlanta (KAYAK route averages; the fastest one-stop where there's no fall nonstop) plus the drive to the house: full marks up to 1.5 hours, minus 1.1 per extra hour, minus 0.5 for a passport, minus 1 for an overnight transatlantic flight, minus 0.3 for each of your cities without a nonstop. Golf, house and weird are judgment calls from the research, anchored to the same scale for every destination.
