1

Las Vegas

To par−3

Every item on your list, within 30 minutes of the house.

Vegas wins on the weird column by a mile, and its golf, weather and nightlife are all strong in mid-October. WalletHub ranks it first in the country for nightlife and second for singles. The locals aren't the athletic crowd (87th of 100 on the 2026 fitness index), but the pool clubs fill with visitors in their 20s and 30s until the season ends in late October.

Golf
Wynn Golf Club (Tom Fazio, on the Strip; caddie, food every four holes and loaner Callaways included; dynamic $450–1,000). Cascata (Rees Jones, Boulder City; about $350–450 plus a required ~$50 caddie). Backup: Paiute's Wolf course (Pete Dye). Shadow Creek doesn't fit: public play is Monday–Thursday for MGM hotel guests.
House
Closest to yours: the Modern 7,000 sq ft Villa on the west side (Airbnb 37182939), 8 BR on a gated acre, with a full basketball and tennis court, pool, spa, casita and high ceilings, 15 minutes from the Strip. Cheaper: Grand Casa (Vrbo 4348055) from $1,734/night, with a hoop and a rooftop deck. See the house shortlist.
Crowd
Encore Beach Club, Tao Beach and LIV Beach run until late October. Circa's Stadium Swim is open all year with college football on a 143-ft screen. Nights: OMNIA, Zouk, LIV, Marquee, Hakkasan.
Weird
Drive a tank over a car at Battlefield Vegas. Dogfight in Extra 330s at Sky Combat Ace. Bulldozers at Dig This. Ferrari laps at Exotics Racing. Helicopter to a champagne landing in the Grand Canyon. Gentlemen's clubs: Sapphire, Spearmint Rhino, Crazy Horse 3.
Window
Oct 7–24 (pools open, 81° average high) or April. Avoid F1 week, Nov 18–20, 2027.
Flights
BOS 6h01 · DC 5h17 · EWR 5h44 · ATL 4h25, all nonstop
The catch
Clark County had approved 209 rental permits against 11,000+ listings as of February 2026, so a legal, party-tolerant 8-bedroom house is the hard part, and most ban outdoor music. You've probably done Vegas before; the itinerary below skips the standard version.
2

Austin & the Hill Country

To parE

The youngest big city on the list, and the only one where you can hunt from a helicopter.

Austin ties the house you're in. It has the shortest flights of the top three and the best crowd numbers of any finalist: median age 34.8, a record 55,000 students at UT, sixth in the country for nightlife and for singles, and 28th on the fitness index. October averages 83° by day and 61° at night. The golf is good rather than great, and the best of it sits behind a resort-guest rule you can solve by holding a couple of hotel rooms.

Golf
Omni Barton Creek, Fazio Canyons (#20 in Texas per GOLF) and Fazio Foothills, both former Golf Digest 100 Greatest Public. Registered Omni guests only; estimated $250–300. Public fallback: Lost Pines Golf Club (formerly Wolfdancer; Arthur Hills, slope 141, about $150 on weekends), 45–50 minutes east and on the way to the airport.
House
Lake Austin: 25–35 minutes to downtown, 15 to Barton Creek. Closest to yours: ABOVE "Elite Estate" on Lake Austin (7 BR, sleeps 16–17, pool and spa, basketball/pickleball court, theater, sand volleyball, boat slip). The splurge: Escondido Chateau (Vrbo 490043), 10 BR on 12.6 acres with 800 ft of Lake Travis frontage, infinity pool, hot tub, two lit pickleball courts, a basketball court and a yacht dock, about $3,803/night, but an hour from downtown.
Crowd
Rainey Street bungalow bars, West 6th for young professionals, the White Horse honky-tonk, and Devil's Cove on Lake Travis, where about 200 boats raft up on Saturdays.
Weird
Helicopter hog hunting out of Temple or Bryan (feral hogs are an invasive pest in Texas, and shooting them from the air is legal). Full-auto at The Range at Austin. Two-step lessons at the Broken Spoke, open since 1964. Gentlemen's clubs: Yellow Rose, Sugar's, Perfect 10 (check they're still operating).
Window
Oct 14–17, 2027 sits between Austin City Limits (expected Oct 1–3 and 8–10) and the F1 U.S. Grand Prix (Oct 22–24). Late March–April also works.
Flights
BOS 4h40 · DC 3h32 · EWR 4h14 · ATL 2h33, all nonstop
The catch
The best courses are resort-gated. COTA's public driving days end in November 2026, when the track becomes a private club. Many big houses ban "bachelor parties," and Austin caps noise at 75 dB.
3

San Diego

To par+2

Perfect weather, the fittest crowd, and a U.S. Open course anyone can book.

October in San Diego averages 75° and almost never rains. The city ranks 11th on the 2026 fitness index, the best of any finalist. Torrey Pines South is a public course that has hosted two U.S. Opens, and one person can book tee times for all eight of you. It gives up ground on the weird column, because California doesn't rent machine guns, and on travel: it's the longest flight in the top three.

Golf
Torrey Pines South (Rees Jones redesign; #40 on Golf Digest's 100 Greatest Public). Non-residents pay $322 Friday–Sunday plus a $50 booking fee and $48 cart. Times release at 7 PM Pacific, 90 days out, and one booker can hold two tee times. Aviara (Arnold Palmer, at the Park Hyatt; about $325). Maderas (Golf Digest 100 Greatest Public; about $200–275).
House
Bird Rock, La Jolla: 5 minutes to Pacific Beach, 15 to Torrey, 20 to the Gaslamp. Example: "Celebrity Oceanfront Estate" (AvantStay; Airbnb 23789760), 8 BR, sleeps 18, heated pool, hot tub, rooftop pickleball/tennis court, theater, gym, wine cellar and a street-legal golf cart, $3,500/night base on Airbnb, city license STR-00454L. Skip Del Mar, which now allows rentals only in primary residences.
Crowd
Pacific Beach for 20s energy (Thursday and Sunday afternoons are big), the Gaslamp on Friday and Saturday (Pendry's Pool House, Oxford Social Club, PARQ), Little Italy rooftops. UCSD and SDSU enroll about 87,000 between them.
Weird
Tandem paragliding off the Torrey Pines cliffs ($200). Skydiving from 13,000 ft ($229). Sportfishing for yellowfin and yellowtail out of Point Loma. A SEALFIT 20X challenge ($449–495, in Temecula). Gentlemen's clubs: Cheetahs, Pacers (California's nude clubs can't serve alcohol).
Window
Late September to mid-October: warmest ocean, driest weeks.
Flights
BOS 6h24 · DC 5h36 · EWR 6h06 · ATL 4h41, all nonstop
The catch
The longest flights of the top three. No full-auto rentals anywhere in California. Weekend Torrey times go within minutes of release, and the tee time can't be transferred: the person who booked it has to show up.
T4

Los Cabos

To par+4

Passport required

The best golf on the list, and staffed villas.

If the group will fly to Mexico, Cabo has the strongest golf-and-villa combination in this report: a course that hosts a PGA Tour event, villas that come with a chef and bartender, and dry 84° days by mid-November. It slipped from fourth to a tie for fourth on travel. Atlanta and DC have nonstops, Newark's run four times a week, and Boston has none.

Golf
Diamante Dunes (Davis Love III; ranked #1 in Mexico) and El Cardonal (Tiger Woods; home of the PGA Tour's World Wide Technology Championship). You can only play them by renting a privately owned Diamante unit for at least three nights, which a Thursday–Sunday trip meets exactly; about $535 a round. Open to anyone: Solmar Golf Links (Greg Norman, $395) and the Cabo del Sol Course (Weiskopf).
House
Closest to yours: Villa Chavez in Pedregal (7–8 BR, sleeps 18, saltwater pool, spa, a private tennis/pickleball court, from about $3,000/night). Most staff: Villa Lands End, with a chef, bartender and "nightlife host" included. Skip Mantea Casa Cabo: 7-night minimum, and all-male groups must pay for a 24-hour butler.
Crowd
Medano Beach by day (Taboo, Mango Deck, SUR Beach House, Bagatelle, Mandala), El Squid Roe and Cabo Wabo at night. Cabo was the #2 international spring-break destination in 2026.
Weird
Striped marlin season opens in November (full-day charters $445–3,500 per boat). A private yacht to El Arco runs about $1,500 for eight. UTVs, ziplines and bungee at Wild Canyon ($298). Whale sharks in La Paz, October–May. Gentlemen's clubs: Splash, Mermaids.
Window
Thu Nov 11 – Sun Nov 14, 2027, or early December. Avoid August–October (hurricane season peaks in September; Bisbee's marlin tournament fills the marina in late October).
Flights
BOS 8h29 (one stop) · DC 5h38 (IAD, 4/wk) · EWR 6h14 (4/wk) · ATL 4h27
The catch
No shooting of any kind. Mexico has no tourist ranges, and bringing in even one round of ammunition can mean 5–30 years in prison, so check every golf bag. The best courses are gated. The State Department rates Baja California Sur Level 2 (exercise increased caution).

Miami

+4

The nightlife ceiling, with a rental problem.

WalletHub ranks Miami #2 in the country for nightlife, and it's 2–3½ hours from all four of your cities. Go mid-November, after the wet season and before Art Basel.

Golf
Doral's Blue Monster ($566–714 with a required forecaddie), the 1925 Donald Ross course at the Biltmore (~$280), Crandon on Key Biscayne (~$225).
Weird
Lock & Load Miami full-auto packages ($259–389), a yacht day at the Haulover sandbar, Everglades airboats.
Catch
Miami Beach and Coral Gables ban short rentals of single-family homes. The houses with courts are 20–30 minutes inland.

Charleston

+5

The best single round in this report, two hours from home.

The Ocean Course at Kiawah (Pete Dye; the 1991 Ryder Cup and two PGA Championships) beats anything else on the list. Charleston is also the closest destination to your four cities. The nightlife is smaller and ends earlier.

Golf
Ocean Course (est. $450–600 plus caddie), Osprey Point (Fazio), Turtle Point (Nicklaus). Book lodging through the resort for preferred tee times.
Weird
Quail hunts and sporting clays on 15,000 acres at Back Woods Quail Club; Gulf Stream fishing; sandbar days by Morris Island Lighthouse.
Catch
No rental here has a basketball court, Kiawah houses top out near 7 BR, and Isle of Palms caps overnight guests at 12.

Riviera Maya

+8

Tulum's crowd with Playa del Carmen's convenience.

Tulum's beach clubs draw the most international crowd on the list, and staffed 8-bedroom villas cost less than Cabo's. Base in Playa del Carmen and make Tulum one big night.

Golf
El Camaleón at Mayakoba (Greg Norman; a cenote in the first fairway; $329–349), Iberostar Playa Paraíso (P.B. Dye; $199).
Weird
Diving with bull sharks off Playa (Nov–Mar, Advanced cert, ~$180), cenote dives, a temazcal sweat lodge, Papaya Playa's full-moon parties.
Catch
Humid, October is the wettest month, and U.S. staff are told to be extra alert after dark in Playa, Tulum and Cancún.

Puerto Rico

+21

Caribbean golf without a passport.

It's under four hours nonstop from all four of your cities, and you never leave the U.S. TPC Dorado Beach East is the island's best course, and Laguna Grande in Fajardo glows blue at night, an hour from San Juan.

Golf
TPC Dorado Beach East (Robert Trent Jones Sr.; #1 in Puerto Rico), Bahia Beach (RTJ Jr., about an hour away).
Weird
Kayak a bioluminescent bay, hike El Yunque rainforest, ride Toro Verde's "El Monstruo" zipline.
Catch

Palm Springs

+21

Golf first, everything else a distant second.

The Coachella Valley has the deepest bench of resort golf here and perfect November weather. It falls apart on the crowd (Palm Springs' median age is 58.5) and on flights: none of your cities flies there nonstop in the fall.

Golf
PGA West Stadium (Pete Dye; the island-green 17th), PGA West Nicklaus Tournament, Indian Wells Players, Desert Willow Firecliff.
Weird

Catch
La Quinta hasn't issued new rental permits outside 18 resort areas since 2021, and courses close for overseeding in October.

Bend, Oregon

+21

The wildcard: three top-100 public courses and a legal psychedelic session.

Bend is the only place on this list where a group psilocybin session is legal under state law. Three Golf Digest 100 Greatest Public courses sit within 30 minutes. The crowd is very fit and in bed by ten.

Golf
Pronghorn Nicklaus (#41; $199–239), Tetherow (David McLay Kidd; #61), Crosswater (#69; Sunriver guests only, $395). Season ends around Nov 1.
Weird
Drop Thesis runs small-group psilocybin sessions ($1,050 low-dose; $1,900 with prep and integration). Oregon allows groups up to 25; everyone needs a one-on-one prep session at least 24 hours before.
Catch
October nights hit 34°, there's no 8-bedroom house with a private pool, and every one of you connects.

The Irish option

Links, whiskey and a red-eye

You asked for Ireland, so I scored the two best Irish versions of this trip the same way as everything else. Both have world-class links golf. Both lose points on 57–62° fall weather and the overnight flight. Dublin finishes 9th; the Causeway Coast misses the cut by one stroke. If Ireland is the call, do Dublin, and do it in early September.

13

The Causeway Coast

To par+22

Passport + UK ETA

The best golf in Ireland, ten minutes from the oldest licensed distillery.

Northern Ireland's north coast has Royal Portrush (fifth in the world, two Opens since 2019) and Portstewart ten minutes apart, Bushmills down the road, and the Giant's Causeway. It misses the cut on travel: there are no U.S. nonstops to Belfast, so you'd fly into Dublin and drive about three hours north. The nightlife is in Belfast, 70 minutes away.

Golf
Royal Portrush Dunluce (£420; visitors Thu and Fri mornings, Thu after 2 PM, Sat after 3 PM, Sun after 2 PM). Portstewart Strand (£335 May–Sep). Castlerock (£250). Royal County Down, #1 in the world, is two hours away; play it Thursday afternoon on the drive up from Dublin.
House
Saltrock House on Portrush harbour: 7 BR, sleeps 14, hot tub, sauna, snooker, a private bar with sea views, £1,100–1,500 a night, 2-night minimum. The closest Irish match to your house.
Whiskey
Bushmills, licensed since 1608: the £40 premium tour is 2½ hours, six whiskies in the Master Distiller's room, and a cask pull.
Weird
The Carrick-a-Rede rope bridge (£16), surf lessons on Portrush's East Strand (from £35), Dunluce Castle, and driven pheasant from October 1 (visiting guns need a firearms certificate).
Flights
Into Dublin (BOS 6h06 · DC 7h00 · EWR 6h36 · ATL 7h34), then ~3h by road
The catch
The drive, Portrush's narrow visitor tee times, a quiet town outside summer, and a £20 UK travel authorization for each of you.

A note on "Scotch"

Scotch only comes from Scotland. Ireland makes Irish whiskey, spelled with an "e," usually triple-distilled, and "single pot still" is a style only Ireland makes. Order a Redbreast and you'll fit right in.

If the group really means Scotch, the Scottish version of this trip is St Andrews plus a day in Speyside. Say the word and I'll score it.

Fine print

What I ruled out, and why

You said everything was on the table. Some of it doesn't survive contact with the law or a four-day schedule.

Ayahuasca: not on this trip

Reputable centers sell multi-night programs only (Soltara's minimum is 5 nights, Rythmia's 7). They screen out people on SSRIs and with some heart conditions, and each ceremony runs all night. The one-night ceremonies sold in Tulum aren't vetted. If the group is serious, make it a separate trip. For a legal session that fits a weekend, Bend's licensed psilocybin centers are the option.

Guns in Mexico: none

Mexico has no tourist shooting ranges, and bringing in even one round of ammunition can mean 5 to 30 years in prison. Check every golf bag and jacket pocket before Cabo or Playa. All the full-auto in this report is in Nevada, Texas and Florida.

California: no machine guns

Civilian full-auto rentals aren't available in San Diego or Palm Springs. Poway Weapons & Gear has 42 lanes of ordinary shooting if someone insists.

Rental rules are the new bouncer

Las Vegas wants a licensed home and usually bans outdoor music. Miami Beach bans rentals under six months in single-family homes. Austin caps noise at 75 dB, and many listings ban "bachelor parties." Isle of Palms allows 12 overnight guests. Tell hosts the truth: it's an annual reunion of grad-school friends.

Desert courses close to overseed

Desert courses close or go cart-path-only for a couple of weeks each fall to overseed (SilverRock was closed Oct 12–30, 2026). Before you lock Vegas dates, ask Cascata and Wynn for their 2027 schedule.

Vegas pools close in late October

Encore Beach Club, Tao Beach and LIV Beach close in late October, and OMNIA's dayclub at the end of September. Circa's Stadium Swim stays open all year. That's why the Vegas plan is Oct 14–17.

Shadow Creek doesn't fit

Public play is Monday to Thursday, for guests of MGM hotels, at $1,250 with a limo and caddie. On a Thursday-arrival trip, it would mean playing straight off the plane.

Massage: licensed only

Book through an app like Soothe, which sends licensed therapists to the house in Las Vegas, Austin, San Diego and Nashville. It doesn't serve Charleston.

How the scores work

Each destination gets six scores out of 10. Weather is calculated from 1991–2020 monthly normals (NOAA for U.S. cities, SMN for Mexico, IMN for Costa Rica). Average highs of 74–84°F get full marks. Every degree hotter costs 0.6, and humid places count 2–3°F hotter once highs pass 80. Every degree cooler costs 0.45, every degree the low falls under 50°F costs 0.12, and rainy or hurricane months cost 0.3 to 3 more. The other five are judgment calls from the research. Golf covers the quality and access of the best two courses within 45 minutes. The crowd score uses fitness rank, median age, nightlife and singles rankings, and campuses. House covers legal 8-bedroom homes with a pool, hot tub and court. Weird is how memorable the best unusual activity is.

Travel is calculated. It starts with the average outbound flight from Boston, DC (the best of DCA, IAD and BWI), Newark and Atlanta: the nonstop block time where a nonstop runs in the fall, otherwise the fastest one-stop. Add the drive from the airport to the house. Anything up to 1.5 hours gets full marks, and every hour beyond that costs 1.1 points. A passport costs 0.5, an overnight transatlantic flight 1, and each of your cities without a nonstop 0.3. Flight times are KAYAK route averages pulled Oct 3, 2026.

The default weights follow your priorities: golf 25%, crowd 20%, house, weather and weird 15% each, travel 10%. "To par" is the gap to Scottsdale under the same weights and month, at one stroke per tenth of a point. The cut is the top ten and ties. Research was done Oct 3, 2026. Prices are 2025–26 published rates and listings; confirm everything before booking.
