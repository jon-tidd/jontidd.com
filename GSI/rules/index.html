        Ground rules

        Fine print

        How the rules get made

        The top five net-positive wagers in each category are adopted, recalculated live. Any single veto kills a wager outright, and every vote is public: there are no secret ballots on this committee.

        Size the stakes like Mickelson

        His rule for Tuesday money games: "enough where you feel it, but it's not going to affect you." Agree on a per-man cap for the weekend before the first tee, and let the shame carry the rest.

        Real numbers only

        Official GHIN indexes, locked a week before the trip, played at USGA allowances. Use the rolling trip handicap so nobody wins three days running, and the Sandbag Tribunal for anyone who beats his number by five.

        Let an app keep the books

        Squabbit is free and runs trip-long leaderboards across Ryder Cup, skins and Stableford. 18Birdies runs Wolf, Vegas, Nassau and dots. Golf GameBook applies handicap allowances automatically. Settle up before you leave the course.

        What didn't make the list

        Real leagues have done all of these, and they're out: a naked walk through Minneapolis (legal exposure), the One Chip Challenge (pulled from sale after a teenager died), a loser covered in peanut butter in a public dog park (strangers' dogs), and any punishment that reaches a man's employer. A forced political profile picture worried one loser's school administration.

        Who can vote

        Everyone Jon invites by email as an Editor can vote. Viewers and public-link visitors can read the page but can't vote. Your vote saves the moment you tap it, and you can change it anytime.
